App name:
Utsurikomi — Film Camera

Short description:
Apply cinematic film looks to your photos.

Full description:
Utsurikomi is a simple photo editing app for creating cinematic film-inspired images.

Choose a photo from your device, preview different looks, and apply a film-style color grade. All image processing takes place locally on your device. You can save the edited photo to your gallery or share it with another app.

Features:
• Five cinematic film-inspired looks
• Film grain controls: subtle, classic, and strong
• Optional classic white and black frames
• Add a custom note and date to the frame
• Fast local photo processing
• Real-time filter preview
• Save edited photos to your gallery
• Share edited photos
• No account required
• No photo upload or tracking

Utsurikomi is designed to make creative photo editing simple and easy to use.
