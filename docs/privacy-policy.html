Utsurikomi Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Utsurikomi is a photo filter app that lets you select an image, apply a film-style look, and save or share the result.

Information we access

Utsurikomi accesses photos only when you choose an image from your device and when you save an edited image to your photo library. The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or other personal information.

How your photos are processed

Photo processing takes place locally on your device. Utsurikomi does not upload your photos or edited images to a server, and the app does not operate a backend service for photo processing.

Saving and sharing

Edited images are saved to your device only when you choose the save action. If you choose Share, the operating system passes the selected image to the app you choose. That receiving app may process the image according to its own privacy policy.

Third-party services

Utsurikomi does not use advertising services, analytics, or user tracking. The app uses operating-system and Flutter-provided components to select, save, and share images.

Data retention

Temporary processing files are stored in the app's private temporary storage while the app is running. The operating system may remove these files. Images saved to your photo library remain under your control.

Children's privacy

Utsurikomi does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
